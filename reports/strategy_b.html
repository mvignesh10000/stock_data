Strategy B: NIFTY expiry-day iron fly

Sell the ATM straddle on NIFTY's weekly expiry day, buy ~₹3 wings, fixed stops, out by 15:15. Five years of true exchange 1-minute data (Nov 2021 – Nov 2026), today's costs, one lot (65 qty). A modest, tested edge, with the limits spelled out below.

LegsBUY the CE and PE whose premium is closest to ₹3 (wings first) · SELL ATM CE + ATM PE
Leg stop50 % above each short leg's entry (SL-limit at the exchange)
After a stopmove the other short leg's stop to its entry price (trail to breakeven)
Overall stop₹3,000 per lot MTM: exit everything
Exit15:15, shorts first, then wings. No re-entry, no target
Orderslimit orders with an 8 % buffer, re-priced every 3 s (market orders are not allowed for API algos)
Marginabout ₹1.6L per lot on expiry day (SPAN + exposure + 2 % expiry ELM)

Limits

  1. Small edge. Expectancy about ₹474 per trade against a worst day of −₹3,911; with doubled slippage the yearly result falls to about ₹17,026 per lot.
  2. Losing years happen. 2023 (record-low VIX, thin premiums) lost ₹16,715; losing runs of 3 months occurred.
  3. NIFTY-specific. The same rules lost on SENSEX (PF 1.1 in 2023-24, 0.6-0.8 since Oct 2024). Treat it as a NIFTY effect, not a law.
  4. ≥ 85 % winning months is not reachable with 4-5 trades a month; nothing tested exceeded about 64 %.
  5. Backtest ≠ live. Fills, latency, rule changes (lot size, ELM, expiry day, settlement) can erase a small edge. Paper-trade first.

Going live

  1. Paper, 8 expiries on your static-IP machine; average entry slippage must stay below ~1 %.
  2. 1 lot live for 3 months (normal daily Upstox token; check the SEBI algo-ID requirement).
  3. 2 lots only if live PF ≥ 1.2 over ≥ 12 expiries.
Stop and re-evaluate if: drawdown from peak exceeds ₹40,000 per lot · 4 losing months in a row · average slippage above 2 % per fill over 8 expiries · an exchange rule change touches expiry day.

Data and method

  • NIFTY weekly options, 1,230 trading days / 259 expiry days, true exchange 1-minute OHLC: Dhan (2021-11 – 2024-09), Breeze (2024-01 – 09), Upstox (2024-10 – 2026-11). Sources agree on overlaps (closes within 1 % on 98 % of minutes, identical expiry dates).
  • Costs at today's rates: brokerage ₹20/order, STT 0.15 % on sells, exchange, SEBI, stamp, 18 % GST. Slippage 0.5 % per fill (min ₹0.10) plus 1 % on stop fills. Stops checked pessimistically on intrabar highs. Lot held at 65 in every year.
  • Rejected along the way: other fly settings selected on 2024-26 (failed both tests), short strangles, breakout and momentum buying, pre-expiry straddles, ORB spreads, calendar spreads (PF 0.36-0.88 everywhere), VIX switching, SENSEX.
