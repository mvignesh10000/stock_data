Verdict (latest)

  • Core strategy: expiry-day iron fly (trading DTE 0, every Tuesday). Sell ATM CE+PE at 09:20, buy OTM wings, 60% leg SL, trail-to-breakeven, ₹2,000 overall SL, exit 15:15.
  • It is partially overfit. The structure has an edge (62% of 432 variants profitable; bootstrap CI of mean P&L > 0), but our pick sits at the top of that distribution, a one-shot 2024→2025-26 test of the simpler procedure failed, and PBO is 16–21%. Plan on ₹8–25k per lot per year, not the full-sample ₹42k. The edge is shrinking.
  • ₹2L: fly with ±400 wings, 1 lot. ₹3L: fly ±400 + the ORB directional hedged spread on expiry day (uncorrelated, positive in 2024 and 2025-26). Paper-trade the Gap-and-Go buy as a third book.
  • Rejected after testing: VIX switching, ORB straddle buying, sideways-day strangles, OI-wall selling, butterflies/Batman/ratios intraday, all non-expiry selling, the Williams back-up, naked straddles.

Rules

  1. Trade only on expiry day (trading DTE 0): Thursday before 2-Sep-2025, Tuesday now.
  2. At 09:20 (AlgoTest time = close of the 09:19 candle) sell the ATM CE and ATM PE.
  3. At the same time buy OTM12 wings on both sides (±600 pts); buys first for margin benefit. (₹2L account: OTM8 / ±400.)
  4. Stop-loss 60% on each sold leg; trail SL to break-even (SL legs) when one leg stops out.
  5. Overall SL ₹2,000 per lot (MTM, candle close). No target, no re-entry.
  6. Exit everything at 15:15. Set AlgoTest “Stop monitoring after” 15:14.

How this was done

Data. Kaggle NIFTY 1-minute option chains: true OHLC for 2024 (verified 99.96% identical to an independent ICICI Breeze source on 3,019,458 bars), LTP for 2025-26 with intra-minute wicks calibrated on true OHLC. India VIX from NSE. The Mendeley set (daily 2017-21) and rahultr1 (daily) were unusable for intraday; the TradyFlow notebook is a US options-flow study.

Engine. Rust, 1-minute bars, AlgoTest candle convention. Leg SL/target on high/low with gap-through fills at the next open; overall SL/lock at candle close; pessimistic same-bar re-check after stops move. Costs: ₹20/order, 0.1% STT, NSE 0.03503%, SEBI, stamp, GST at today's rates and lot 65. Margin: scenario SPAN + exposure + SEBI 2% expiry-day ELM, calibrated to the exchange SPAN file. Trading-day DTE.

Search & validation. v1–v4 superseded; v5 32,610-trial search rejected; v6/v7 pre-declared grids with plateau selection and anchored quarterly walk-forward; v8 VIX framework; v9 overfit audit (PBO/CSCV, one-shot split, bootstrap), compounding, regime/OI study, Varsity structures, Gap-and-Go replication.
