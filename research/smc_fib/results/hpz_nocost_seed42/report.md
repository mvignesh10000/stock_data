## Caveats

- The paper gives no exact rules, event definition, base rate or costs. Rules here are our reading (see the plan); the paper's 72 % is compared against controls, not taken as given.
- HPZ score = P_F * P_L with P_L = 1 inside an active order block or liquidity pool. max over the bar's price range inside the zone, as in the plan.
- Stock daily shorts are not possible in cash delivery; short trades on daily bars are theoretical (futures-only names). Read the long-only rows for the tradable version.
- Stock daily costs (46 bps total) are applied to daily trades; intraday MIS costs (13 bps) to 15-min trades; index 5 bps.
- Sector robustness not run: the data has no sector mapping.
- The walk-forward selects on train trades only; the OOS years are not used to choose parameters. The reference cell for E1 and E2 was fixed before running (k=2, d=1.0, N=100, variant A, tau=0.5, c=0.5).
- Portfolio metrics use equal-capital sleeves: each symbol has its own sleeve, risks 1 % of it per trade (one position at a time), and the portfolio return is the average over all sleeves. Stocks and the index are pooled this way; no margin or capacity limits.
