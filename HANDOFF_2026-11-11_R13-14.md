## 6. Strategy M: exact rules (M-safe)

* Reference: NIFTY spot at 09:20 (close of the 09:19 candle). Signal at 15:19: r = spot / spot(09:20) - 1.
* **r > +0.3 %** and spot > close 5 trading days ago: buy PE 3 % of spot below the ATM (wing, first), then sell ATM PE.
  **r < -0.3 %** and spot < close 5 trading days ago: buy CE 3 % above, then sell ATM CE. Otherwise no trade.
* Contract: nearest weekly expiry after today (tdte >= 1; on expiry day use next week's contract; the night before expiry uses the expiring one).
* Product D (carry-forward). Limit orders with an 8 % price buffer (market orders not allowed for algos), repriced every 3 s.
* Exit 09:20 next trading day, short leg first, then the wing. No overnight stop. Never carried into the session.
* Kill-switch (executor): live drawdown above Rs 90k per lot, or last 12 months negative with at least 6 months of live history.
* Backtest assumes the 5-day close is the 15:19 print; the executor uses the previous daily closes (a difference of a few points).
* Weekly routine: M every trading day (signal 15:19:30, exit 09:20); W enters Wednesday 15:15 for a Tuesday expiry; on expiry morning W and M
  exit at 09:20 and then B enters. Margin does not stack: W 1.1L + M 0.69L overnight (about 1.8L); B 2 lots 3.3-3.4L on expiry day after the others exit.
* Executor flags: `--lots`, `--account`, `--legs both|up`, `--no-align`, `--buffer`, `--live`, `--now-entry up|dn` and `--exit-now` (paper tests).
  Files: `data/upstox/m_state_<acct>.json`, `m_ledger_<acct>.csv`, `m_<acct>.log`, `m_ref_<acct>.json`.
  Schedule it at 09:12 IST every trading day. Live orders need a normal daily Upstox token and the user's registered static IP (not available here).

## 7. Risks and caveats (do not skip)

1. **Overnight gaps against the signal cannot be stopped.** Worst adverse nights since 2015 for M's signal: 20-Mar-2020 up signal then +7.25 % adverse
   (about Rs 114k naked at today's level), 23-Mar-2020 5.07 %, 13-Mar-2020 4.57 %, 30-Apr-2020 4.49 %, 3-Apr-2020 4.02 %, 8-Nov-2016 3.91 %,
   21-Aug-2015 3.34 %, 23-Jun-2016 2.98 %. 3.8 % of signal nights moved more than 1 % against, 0.9 % more than 2 %. The 3 % wing caps the loss
   near Rs 42k per lot (not Rs 60k as stated in the first version of the card for a 4 % wing).
2. **The threshold, the short-option structure and the trend filter were chosen after seeing 2021-26**; that period is not a clean holdout for M.
   The clean evidence is the index signal (11 years, four blocks) and the 2017-20 option run (the filter was added after that run too; its
   2017-20 improvement is therefore also partly in-sample for the filter idea).
3. **Wing prices before 2024 cannot be tested** (2017-20 data holds ATM +/- 500 points only; Dhan 2021-23 far strikes are stale/sparse). All
   wing-inclusive numbers are 2024-26: 2.85 years, about 200 trades.
4. **The down leg is weaker evidence than the up leg.** Index: down-aligned +4.1 bp (t 1.1); futures short after down days lost money 2015-2021. In
   options the down leg made money in all six calendar years of 2021-26 and in 2018-2020. The up leg alone is the safest single-leg version.
5. **Wing cost varies:** the 3 % put cost Rs 39 with a week to expiry (paper test, 10-Nov-2026), only a few rupees near expiry. This makes
   the wing a real cost, about 30 % of M's profit.
6. **Regime dependence.** 2017-18 barely positive for unfiltered M; 2024 for M-safe about flat (+4k). Expect flat years.
7. **Execution at the day's edges:** signal at 15:19, orders at 15:20; exits at 09:20 have wider spreads than midday. Slippage x2 still leaves PF 1.34
   naked. A missed exit turns the trade into an unplanned day position. 1-s bid/ask recorder is built but not deployed, so real spreads are unmeasured.
8. **Simulator facts:** lot size 65 for all years (history had 25/50/75); settlement at last-30-min average approximated by the last bar's close;
   2026-11-04..06 are labelled with the 9-Nov expiry (data caveat from the previous handoff); one missing expiry (26-Dec-2024).
9. **Rule changes** (expiry weekday, lot size, STT, margin, SEBI retail-algo registration from 1-Apr-2026) change every trade. Check Upstox's
   Algo-ID requirement before going live.
10. Everything above is backtest; no live money has been traded. Position sizing should follow the bootstrap p95 drawdown, not the average.
